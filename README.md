# Customer & Sales Analysis of a Global Electronics Retailer
## Project Background
This research analyzes consumer behavior and sales data from a simulated global electronics retailer. The dataset includes comprehensive records of product details, purchase transactions, consumer demographics, and store performance in a variety of locations and currencies.

The objective of this project is to clean, explore, and analyze the data to uncover meaningful business insights related to customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and sales performance. The analysis was performed using Python in Google Colab for data cleaning and transformation, while SQLite was used to run analytical SQL queries to efficiently extract insights.

## Key Analysis Areas
### Customer Analysis
- Demographic Distribution: Analysis of customer profiles based on gender, age (derived from birthdate), and geographic location (city, state, country, and continent).
- Purchasing Patterns: Identification of customer purchasing behavior, including average order value, purchase frequency, and preferred products.

### Sales Analysis
- Overall Sales Performance: Evaluation of total sales over time to identify trends and potential seasonality.
- Sales by Product: Identification of top-performing products based on revenue and quantity sold.
- Sales by Store: Comparison of store-level performance to assess differences in sales contributions.
- Sales by Currency: Analysis of sales distribution across different currencies, taking exchange rates into account.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python (Google Colab) – data cleaning, feature engineering, and exploratory analysis
- SQLite – querying and aggregating data for insight generation
- Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib/Seaborn – data manipulation and visualization
